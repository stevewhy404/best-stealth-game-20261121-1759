The Quiet Gallery — a top-down stealth game. You are Wren, a thief slipping through a lamplit gallery at night: stay out of the wardens' golden vision cones, hide behind crates and vanish inside the tall ferns, toss pebbles to lure guards away, steal every moon-lantern relic and reach the green exit unseen. Three galleries with patrols to learn, a detection meter that gives you a chance to recover, lamp light that makes you easy to spot, and footsteps that tell you where the guards are.

## Run it

```sh
cd game && python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Move with WASD or arrow keys, hold Shift to sneak, click to toss a pebble, P to pause, R to restart, M to mute.
